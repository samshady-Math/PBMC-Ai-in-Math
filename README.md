# AI in Math

This repository serves as the hub for the University of Michigan’s Proof Based Math Club’s newest initiative: **AI in Math**. Our primary goal is to explore how artificial intelligence can support the development and understanding of mathematical proofs. Alongside this, we aim to give members an opportunity to gain hands-on experience with Lean and mathematical proof formalization.

Through collaborative projects, we hope to investigate how AI can help generate proof ideas, translate mathematical arguments into Lean, and support the process of formal verification. We are interested in both the possibilities and limitations of these tools, including how to evaluate AI-generated arguments for correctness.

This repository will bring together our code, formalized proofs, learning resources, and project notes as the initiative develops. Whether members are new to Lean or already have experience, our goal is to create a welcoming space to learn, experiment, and explore the intersection of AI and mathematics.
